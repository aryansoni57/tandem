Tandem icon concepts

Six adaptive-icon foregrounds (108×108, art inside the 66×66 safe zone), each shown on three masks plus the monochrome themed-icon layer at 48 and 24 px.

1

Side By Side

A laptop and a phone stand shoulder to shoulder as one paired unit, the phone slightly taller like a partner leaning in.

2

Interlocked Twin Rings

Two rings woven over and under each other like chain links, so neither device can slip away from the other.

3

Two Halves Meet

Two offset half-circles slide together around a shared core dot, completing one whole only when both are present.

4

Bicycle For Two

An abstract tandem bicycle: two wheels joined by one zigzag frame with two seats, two riders moving as one.

5

Rounded Hand Clasp

Two rounded C-shaped hooks reach into each other like a handshake, each holding the other in place.

6

Phone Laptop T

A T monogram where a laptop seen edge-on forms the crossbar and a phone outline forms the stem, hinged together.
